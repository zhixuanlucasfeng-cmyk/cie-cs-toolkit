Cambridge pseudocode, written and run

            
Write a program, run it against test cases you cannot see, then read the
              trace line by line until you find the step that was wrong. Built for IGCSE 0478 and
              AS & A Level 9618.

            

              
              
            

            
Everything stays in this browser. There is nothing to sign in to, and it
              keeps working with the network off.

          

          

            
 Why one right answer is not enough

            

            

          

        


        

          

            
What it does

            
    
              
  • An editor with syntax highlighting, live error checking and breakpoints.
    • 
              
  • Marking that runs your program against hidden test cases and reports what each one did.
    • 
              
  • A name for the mistake when you get one wrong: a boundary slip, negatives left
                unhandled, a loop that runs one time too many.
    • 
              
  • Whatever you get wrong comes back on a spaced schedule until it stops being wrong.
    • 
              
  • Structured English, trace tables, flowcharts and logic circuits, converted between each other.
    • 
            

          

          

            
What it covers

            

          

        


        

          
For teachers

          
Students practise in the browser without signing in to anything, and
            export the evidence for you to read.

          

            

              
How it works

              
    
                
  • Send the link. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
    • 
                
  • Students take a short diagnostic, then practise against what it found.
    • 
                
  • They export JSON or CSV and hand it to you; every row names the course it came from.
    • 
                
  • The questions are original, written against published syllabus content.
    • 
              

            

            

              
Where it stops

              
    
                
  • Coverage is partial. The list above is counted from the question bank rather than claimed.
    • 
                
  • There is no class dashboard, so evidence reaches you as exported files.
    • 
                
  • Progress lives in each student's browser and is lost if they clear it.
    • 
                
  • None of the questions are past-paper material, and nothing here predicts a grade.
    •

About DryRun

        
The same algorithm can be structured English, pseudocode, a trace table, a flowchart or a logic circuit. Understanding usually breaks when you move between two of them. DryRun lets you move across all five and see where the versions stop agreeing.

      


      

        
Your data

        

          
Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and the page carries no
            analytics and no third-party scripts. Signed out, once it has loaded it makes no network
            requests at all — and it is signed out unless you deliberately sign in.

          
Unless you sign in. Signing in is optional and nothing asks you to.
            If you do, your progress record is copied to the sync server so your other devices can read it, and your
            email address is stored so the sign-in link can reach you. Nothing else is sent: no browsing, no keystrokes,
            no third-party tracking. Delete account and synced data in Progress removes the stored copy and the
            email address for good; the copy in this browser stays.

          
Stored: the course you chose, every attempt and its marks, your topic evidence, your review queue, your saved editor program, and any feedback you write. All under dryrun.store.v3 and a few keys beside it, including whether you chose paper or night.

          
The last few versions of your work are kept as automatic copies, and one is taken before anything risky. A damaged record is restored from the newest copy by itself.

          
If you clear your browser data, switch device or use private browsing, your progress is gone unless you exported it. Settings has Export, Import and Erase. Erasing everything removes the automatic copies too.

          
Several people can share one browser: add each of them in Settings and they get separate records. Names are labels typed on the device, not accounts.

        

      


      

        
For schools

        

          
Because nothing leaves the browser, there is no student data for a school to process on
            our side. Evidence reaches a teacher only when a student exports a file and hands it over.
